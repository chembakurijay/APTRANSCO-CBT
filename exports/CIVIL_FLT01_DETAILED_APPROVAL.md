**Q1** | Topic: Axial Stress | Pattern: Numerical | Diff: Easy | PYQ: Inspired

A circular steel rod of diameter 20 mm carries an axial tensile load of 62.8 kN. The axial stress in the rod is closest to:

(A) 200 MPa  
(B) 100 MPa  
(C) 400 MPa  
(D) 50 MPa  

**Answer: A**  
**Formula:** σ = P/A  
**Dual-method:**  
- M1: A=π(20)²/4=314.16 mm²; σ=62800/314.16≈200 MPa  
- M2: σ=4P/(πd²)=4×62800/(π×400)≈200 MPa  
**Distractors:** B = used d not d² (half area error inverted); C = used radius as diameter; D = used cm² inconsistently.
